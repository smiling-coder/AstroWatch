MISSION OBJECTIVES

Problems We Aim to Understand

AstroWatch turns complex space-safety concepts into interactive learning experiences.

01

Orbital Complexity

Problem: Orbital paths can be difficult to understand.

Approach: Visualise hypothetical trajectories around Earth in an interactive 3D scene.

02

Close Approaches

Problem: Two objects may pass close to each other in space.

Approach: Compare separation and relative speed using clearly labelled example scenarios.

03

Data Uncertainty

Problem: Orbital measurements and predictions have uncertainties.

Approach: Add uncertainty ranges and source timestamps when real orbital datasets are integrated.

04

Scientific Accessibility

Problem: Astronomy datasets can be difficult for beginners to interpret.

Approach: Present object properties, orbital concepts, and scientific explanations in one dashboard.
